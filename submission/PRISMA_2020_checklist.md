**Honest notes for reviewers.** Items 11, 14, 15, 22, and 24a are the known
departures from a full effectiveness-review protocol. They follow from the
study's design as a determinants-framework synthesis (not a meta-analysis of a
single intervention) and from constraints stated openly in the Limitations:
the search covers two databases (institutional access to Scopus/WoS/CINAHL was
unavailable), the review was not prospectively registered, and no formal
per-study risk-of-bias or GRADE certainty rating was applied. These are
disclosed rather than obscured.
