\section{Coding Schema}

The coding schema operationalizes the adoption model:

\begin{equation}
Adoption = f(U, T, I, O)
\end{equation}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{p{2.2cm}p{4.5cm}p{6.5cm}}
\toprule
Domain & Construct & Example Codes \\
\midrule
U & User Readiness & usability, satisfaction, workflow fit, training, trust, perceived usefulness, clinician burden \\
T & Technical Interoperability & system quality, information quality, semantic interoperability, syntactic interoperability, interface reliability, data completeness \\
I & Institutional Alignment & regulation, policy incentives, HITECH, Cures Act, HIPAA, information governance, national digital health strategy \\
O & Organizational Execution & leadership, implementation strategy, change management, stakeholder engagement, vendor governance, resource capacity \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\caption{U--T--I--O Coding Schema}
\label{tab:coding-schema}
\end{table}
