\begin{itemize}

\item \textbf{E2 --- Wrong outcome} --- did not report adoption, implementation,
  barriers, facilitators, or determinants.
\item \textbf{E3 --- Wrong setting} --- non-healthcare IT context without healthcare
  application.
\item \textbf{E4 --- Insufficient methodology} --- opinion/editorial or narrative
  piece without full methodology.
\item \textbf{E5 --- Duplicate} --- duplicate of an already-included or
  already-screened record (better source retained).
\item \textbf{E6 --- Not retrievable} --- full text unavailable after library and
  interlibrary loan requests (reported separately in the PRISMA flow).
\end{itemize}
