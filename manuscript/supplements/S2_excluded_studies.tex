\section*{Supplementary File S2: Full-Text Articles Excluded, with Reasons}
\label{sec:S2}

\noindent\textbf{Purpose:} Provide the per-record audit trail for the 48 reports
assessed for eligibility but excluded, as required for PRISMA 2020 transparency.
Records are drawn from the executed screening log; the full record-level data,
including titles, identifiers, and the basis of assessment (retrieved full text
vs.\ abstract), are provided in the accompanying data file
\texttt{fulltext\_exclusions.csv}.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Exclusion-reason legend} (Stage-2 / full-text scope codes):
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{X1 --- Patient/consumer-facing only} --- study of individual
  patients' or consumers' acceptance, attitudes, or use of a portal/app/tool,
  with no organizational or system-level adoption lens.
\item \textbf{X2 --- Single-condition or single-intervention tool} --- a specific
  digital-health intervention for one clinical condition, evaluated for clinical
  effect or feasibility rather than system adoption.
\item \textbf{X3 --- Wrong outcome} --- uses an EHR/health-IT system as a data
  source or delivery channel to study a clinical/disease outcome, algorithm, or
  cost, rather than studying adoption of the system itself.
\item \textbf{X4 --- Out-of-scope technology/setting} --- not an
  EHR/EMR/HIE/health-IT-infrastructure system, or a non-healthcare setting.
\item \textbf{X5 --- Insufficient methodology / publication type} --- editorial,
  letter, commentary, protocol-only, or abstract-only.
\end{itemize}
