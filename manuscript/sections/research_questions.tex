\section{Research Questions}
\begin{itemize}
\item RQ1: What determinants most significantly influence eHealth systems adoption?
\item RQ2: How are these determinants reflected in national eHealth implementations?
\item RQ3: How can these determinants be integrated into a broadly applicable adoption model?
\item RQ4: Can the resulting model be operationalized into a bounded, reproducible index that scores an implementation's adoption readiness?
\end{itemize}

\noindent The U-T-I-O Model is developed to serve two related purposes: as a \textit{descriptive framework} it identifies and organizes the determinants that most significantly influence adoption (RQ1--RQ3), and as an \textit{evaluative instrument} it is operationalized into a scored alignment index that diagnoses where a given implementation is strong or constrained (RQ4). Both purposes are addressed in the Results.
