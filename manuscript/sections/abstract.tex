\begin{abstract}
The adoption of interoperable eHealth systems remains uneven despite sustained global investment. This study develops a consolidated adoption model through a PRISMA-based systematic review and multi-case analysis of public health system implementations. Findings are synthesized into an operational model capturing user, technology, institutional, and organizational determinants. The results provide actionable guidance for health system leaders and establish a foundation for future AI-enabled diagnostic integrity research.
\end{abstract}
