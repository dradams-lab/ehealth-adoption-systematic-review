\section{Ethical and Compliance Considerations}
This study used only publicly available and de-identified data sources. No human subjects were involved, and therefore institutional review board (IRB) approval was not required.

Data usage aligns with:
\begin{itemize}
\item HIPAA de-identification standards (45 CFR \S164.514)
\item HITECH interoperability objectives
\item NIST Risk Management Framework principles (SP 800-37, SP 800-53)
\end{itemize}

No protected health information (PHI) was accessed, stored, or analyzed during this study.
