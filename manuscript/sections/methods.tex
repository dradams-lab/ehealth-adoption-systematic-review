\section{Methodology}

\subsection{Systematic Review (PRISMA)}
A systematic review was conducted across PubMed, IEEE Xplore, and Scopus, covering literature published between 2015 and 2026. The review followed PRISMA guidelines to ensure transparency and reproducibility.

\subsection{Inclusion Criteria}
\begin{itemize}
\item Peer-reviewed studies
\item Focus on eHealth, EHR, HIE, or interoperability
\item Empirical, systematic review, or framework-based research
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Exclusion Criteria}
\begin{itemize}
\item Opinion-only studies
\item Non-healthcare systems
\item Studies involving identifiable human subject data
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Multi-Case Analysis}
The study incorporates a comparative analysis of four publicly documented implementations:
\begin{itemize}
\item U.S. ONC Health Information Exchange (HIE) programs
\item UK National Health Service (NHS) Digital Health initiatives
\item Estonia national eHealth system
\item U.S. VA/DoD interoperability programs
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Analytical Model}
\begin{equation}
Adoption = f(U, T, I, O)
\end{equation}

Where:
\begin{itemize}
\item U = User Readiness
\item T = Technical Interoperability
\item I = Institutional Alignment
\item O = Organizational Execution
\end{itemize}
